START HERE ? EASY PORTFOLIO UPDATES

1. Open portfolio.js. This is your content file.
2. Change the text between quotation marks. Keep commas and brackets.
3. Save and refresh index.html in your browser.

PHOTO
Copy your picture into src/assets/profile.png, then set:
  photo: "src/assets/profile.png",

CV
Copy your PDF into src/assets/resume.pdf, then set:
  resume: "src/assets/resume.pdf",
Leave resume empty to hide the CV button.

PROJECTS AND SKILLS
Copy an existing object in the projects or skills list to add another.
Delete or reorder objects to remove or reorder cards/groups.
No layout edits are required.

LINKS
Edit profile.email and profile.github once; all contact links update.
Give each project a real url, or leave it empty for an email link.

Do not edit content.js or script.js for everyday updates.
The original static index.html is a fallback when JavaScript is disabled.
See README.md for examples. Nothing is published automatically.

CERTIFICATES
Place your PDF/image files in src/assets/certificates/.
In portfolio.js, uncomment and fill the example in certificates.
Copy the object to add more cards. Extra cards appear under View all certificates.
